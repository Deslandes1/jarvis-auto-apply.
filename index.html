📎 File Attachments Need EmailJS Paid Plan

JARVIS uses EmailJS to send your applications. EmailJS Free does not support file attachments. To send your CV and certificates as actual files, you need an EmailJS plan that supports attachments.

EmailJS Attachment Limits:
🆓 Free: No attachments
📎 Personal ($9/mo): up to 500 KB
📎 Professional ($15/mo): up to 2 MB
📎 Business ($40/mo): up to 30 MB

❓ How to Use JARVIS Auto-Apply

Send your first application in under 5 minutes.

1
Enter your email address. This is where recruiter replies will land. Your plan is tied to this email.
2
Pick target recruiters. Click "➕" on any recruiter in the sidebar, or click "📝 Edit All" to replace the whole list at once. You can also type your own addresses into "Target Emails".
3
Write your cover letter. Edit the default letter with your own story and skills.
4
Upload CV & certificates. File names appear in the email body. For real attachments, you need an EmailJS paid plan or paste a Google Drive link in your cover letter.
5
Click "Send Applications". Recruiters will reply directly to your email. Check your inbox for responses.
