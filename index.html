❓ How to Use JARVIS Auto-Apply

Send your first application in under 5 minutes.

1
Enter your email address. This is where recruiter replies will land. Your plan is tied to this email.
2
Pick target recruiters. Use the recruiter list in the sidebar or type your own addresses into "Target Emails". Separate multiple emails with commas.
3
Write your cover letter. Edit the default letter with your own story and skills.
4
Upload CV & certificates. File names appear in the email body. For real attachments, paste a Google Drive link in your cover letter.
5
Click "Send Applications". Recruiters will reply directly to your email. Check your inbox for responses.
